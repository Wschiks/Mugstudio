Surf Tycoon: common questions

Surf Tycoon

Where is my progress saved?
On your own device only. There is no account and no server. If you delete the app, clear its data or use “Start over” in the menu, your progress is gone and we cannot bring it back.
I bought something but it is not there. What now?
Open the shop in the game and tap “Restore purchases”. That brings back “Remove ads”, “Coins x5” and your Surf Club membership. Gems that were bought and spent cannot be restored. Refunds are handled by the App Store or Google Play.
How do I cancel the Surf Club subscription?
Cancel it in the settings of your App Store or Google Play account, at least 24 hours before the renewal date. You keep the benefits until the paid period ends. We cannot cancel it for you.
Why do my zones not earn while the game is closed?
Hire a manager for the zone. Away time earns for 2 hours to start with; skills in the Beach tree make that longer, and so does the Surf Club.
What data does the game collect?
None ourselves. Only the optional video ads in the phone apps are shown by Google AdMob. Read the privacy policy.

Dobbelen: common questions

Do I need an account or internet?
No. Dobbelen is played on one phone that you pass around. There is no account, no ads, and it works offline.
Can I play in Dutch?
Yes. The game comes in English and Dutch.
Is any data collected?
No. The game collects nothing and makes no network requests.
